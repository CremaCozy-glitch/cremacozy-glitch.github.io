Maybe the machine is flashing a light at you. Maybe you just realized it's been a while since anything got a proper clean. Either way, you're in the right place, and it's not as big a job as it looks. Most of it is small stuff that takes a minute. The one bigger job, the cleaning cycle, mostly runs on its own.

I use this machine too, so I've written this the way I'd explain it to a friend. Every step comes from Breville's instruction book for the BES870, the standard Barista Express. Other Barista models can have different buttons, so check your own manual if yours isn't this one. Where the book doesn't say something, I'll tell you instead of making it up.

## One light, two different jobs

Look at the CLEAN / DESCALE light on the front first. It means two things depending on how it behaves.

- **Flashing** means the machine wants a cleaning cycle with a cleaning tablet. That's most of this page.
- **Solid** (on and not blinking) means it wants a descale. That's a separate job with its own steps, and we have a whole [descaling guide]({{ '/how-to-descale-breville-barista-express/' | relative_url }}) for it.

If your descaler is running low, the <a href="https://link.amazon/B0afe1IzA" target="_blank" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener">Breville descaling powder on Amazon</a> is the one the manual asks for.

The manual says the cleaning cycle is separate from descaling, so don't treat one as a substitute for the other. If the light is still flashing after you've cleaned, the [Clean Me light fix]({{ '/breville-barista-express-clean-me-light-wont-turn-off-fix/' | relative_url }}) is the place to go next.

## A quick word on safety

Switch the machine off and unplug it before you take any parts apart, and let it cool down first. Never put the machine, the cord or the plug in water. Wash parts by hand with warm water and a mild dish soap, and skip scratchy pads and cloths. None of the parts go in the dishwasher.

## What you need for the cleaning cycle

The first three of these came in the box with the machine:

- one cleaning tablet
- the cleaning disc
- the 1 CUP filter basket
- a large container, like a big bowl or jug (anything that holds a few cups of water will do)

If you're out of tablets, you can find <a href="https://link.amazon/B0aFaDyb2" target="_blank" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener">Breville cleaning tablets on Amazon</a>. Our [cleaning tablets guide]({{ '/best-cleaning-tablets-breville-barista-express/' | relative_url }}) goes through the options in more detail.

## The cleaning cycle, step by step

Do this when the light is flashing. The manual doesn't give a calendar schedule, so let the light tell you.

1. **Load the portafilter.** Put the 1 CUP filter basket in the portafilter. Put the cleaning disc on top of it, then one cleaning tablet on the disc.
2. **Lock it in.** Lock the portafilter into the group head, the spot at the front where it twists in. Put the large container underneath the spouts.
3. **Check the tank and the tray.** The water tank should have cold water in it, and the drip tray should be empty.
4. **Switch the machine off.** Press the POWER button.
5. **Start the cycle.** Press the 1 CUP and 2 CUP buttons together, then press POWER as well. Keep all three held down for about 10 seconds. Count it out if you need to.
6. **Let it run.** The cycle lasts about 5 minutes. The manual says water flows out of the spouts on and off during this time. When it's done you'll hear 2 beeps. That's what mine does too.
7. **Check the tablet.** Take out the portafilter and make sure the tablet has dissolved completely. If it hasn't, run the cycle again until it has.

## Everyday cleaning

These are the small jobs. None of them takes long.

**Portafilter and filter baskets.** Rinse them under hot water right after you use them. That washes off the coffee oils. If a hole in the basket gets blocked, push it clear with the pin on the end of the cleaning tool. Still blocked? Dissolve a cleaning tablet in hot water, soak the basket and the portafilter for about 20 minutes, then rinse them well.

**Steam wand.** Clean it every time you steam milk. Wipe the wand with a damp cloth, point the tip over the drip tray, and run steam for a moment to clear out any milk left inside. If a hole in the tip is blocked, put the dial on STANDBY, switch the machine off and let it cool. Then clear the hole with the pin on the cleaning tool. If it's still blocked, take the tip off with the spanner built into the cleaning tool. Soak it in hot water, clear the holes with the pin, and put it back on.

**Group head and shower screen.** Wipe the inside of the group head and the shower screen with a damp cloth to get rid of loose grounds. Now and then, put an empty basket and portafilter in, hold down 1 CUP, and let a short flow of water rinse out leftover coffee.

**The silicone seal.** Around the shower screen there's a silicone seal that wears out over time. If espresso leaks around the edge of the portafilter, the manual lists a worn seal as one possible cause. The Allen key in your box is for changing it, and the manual has the steps. Breville Consumer Support can help with replacement seals.

**Drip tray and storage tray.** Empty the drip tray whenever the EMPTY ME! indicator shows. To wash it, lift off the grid and lift out the coffee grind separator. Throw away the grounds, then wash the parts in warm soapy water with a soft cloth. Rinse and dry everything. The EMPTY ME! indicator pulls out too, once you release the side latches. The storage tray behind the drip tray comes out and wipes clean with a soft damp cloth.

**Outside of the machine.** Wipe the outer housing and the cup warming tray with a soft damp cloth, then polish with a soft dry one. No abrasive cleaners or pads.

**Water tank and filter.** The manual says to replace the water daily, and it recommends cold filtered water. It doesn't recommend distilled or demineralized water. The filter in the tank helps prevent scale, and changing it every two months means you'll need to descale less often. Change it sooner if you have hard water. For a new filter, soak it in a cup of water for 5 minutes and rinse it under cold running water. Then set the date dial two months ahead. The manual also says to take the filter out if you use filtered or bottled water. Water filters come from Breville Consumer Support, according to the manual.

## Cleaning the grinder

Coffee oil builds up on the grinder burrs too, and the manual says that can get in the way of performance. It has a cleaning routine for it but no schedule, so it's up to you when to do it.

1. Unlock the hopper and empty the beans out. Hold the hopper over a container and turn its release dial.
2. Put the hopper back and lock it.
3. Run the grinder until it's empty.
4. Unlock and remove the hopper again.
5. Unlock the top burr and take it out.
6. Brush the top burr, the lower burr and the grind chute with the cleaning brush from your accessories.

Then put the top burr back and lock it, and refit and lock the hopper. The cleaning steps use the brush only. The manual lists moisture in the grinder as one cause of no coffee coming out, so I'd keep water away from it. The manual also warns that the burrs are extremely sharp, so go slowly.

If the chute is actually blocked, that's a bigger job. The manual has an advanced cleaning routine for it, and we cover it in our [grinder not working guide]({{ '/barista-express-grinder-not-working/' | relative_url }}).

## Quick reference

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Rinse portafilter and filter baskets | Right after each use |
| Wipe and clear the steam wand | After steaming milk |
| Empty the drip tray | When EMPTY ME! shows |
| Cleaning cycle with a tablet | When the CLEAN / DESCALE light is flashing |
| Descale | When the CLEAN / DESCALE light is solid |
| Change the water filter | Every two months, sooner with hard water |
| Replace the water in the tank | Daily |
| Clean the grinder burrs | No schedule in the manual |
