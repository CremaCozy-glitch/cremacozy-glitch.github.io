If your espresso starts tasting harsher than usual, shots run unevenly, or the machine just feels overdue for maintenance, it may be time to backflush your Breville Barista Express.

Backflushing is one of the most important cleaning routines for this machine. It helps remove coffee oils and residue from the group head and internal valve system so your shots stay cleaner and your machine keeps working properly.

In this guide, I’ll show you how to backflush a Breville Barista Express step by step, how often to do it, and the difference between a water backflush and a tablet backflush.

## What does backflushing do?

Backflushing pushes water back through the brew path using the rubber cleaning disc in the portafilter. This helps loosen and remove built-up coffee oils and residue inside the machine.

A proper backflush helps:

- keep espresso flavor cleaner
- reduce rancid coffee oil buildup
- maintain smoother operation of the brew valve
- support long-term machine maintenance

It is a simple task, but it makes a big difference over time.

## What you need

Before starting, gather these items:

- the Breville cleaning disc (rubber insert)
- 1 Breville cleaning tablet or compatible espresso machine cleaning tablet
- the portafilter
- a cloth for wiping up water
- access to fresh water in the machine reservoir

If you are only doing a quick rinse backflush, you can skip the tablet and use water only.

## How to backflush the Breville Barista Express

Here is the regular cleaning method I use when doing a full backflush cycle.

### 1. Turn on the machine

Make sure the machine is on and fully heated before starting.

### 2. Insert the cleaning disc

Place the rubber cleaning disc into the single-shot filter basket inside the portafilter.

### 3. Add a cleaning tablet

Put one cleaning tablet in the center of the cleaning disc.

### 4. Lock the portafilter into the group head

Insert and lock the portafilter into the machine just like you are preparing to brew espresso.

### 5. Start the cleaning cycle

On the Breville Barista Express, the cleaning cycle is typically triggered by pressing the **1 Cup** and **2 Cup** buttons together while the machine is off, then pressing the **Power** button. On some units, the **Clean Me** light will indicate when this is needed.

If your model is already on and you are doing a manual backflush, you can run short brew cycles manually instead.

### 6. Let the cycle run

The machine will pulse water through the system and release pressure in stages. This helps dissolve and flush out coffee oils and residue.

### 7. Wait for the cycle to finish

Let the automatic cleaning cycle complete fully before removing the portafilter.

### 8. Remove and rinse the portafilter

Take out the portafilter carefully. Rinse the portafilter, cleaning disc, and basket thoroughly.

### 9. Run a rinse cycle with water only

Reinsert the cleaning disc without a tablet and run another cycle to flush out any remaining cleaner.

### 10. Repeat if needed

If the machine has gone a long time without cleaning, a second rinse cycle can help clear everything out.

### 11. Remove the portafilter, discard the tablet residue, and rinse everything well.

### 12. Wipe down the group head area

Use a clean cloth to wipe around the group head and remove any loosened residue.

## How to backflush without a tablet

If you just want a quick maintenance rinse between deeper cleanings, you can do a water-only backflush.

### Steps:

1. Insert the cleaning disc into the portafilter.
2. Lock the portafilter into the group head.
3. Run a short brew cycle for around 10 seconds.
4. Stop the shot and let pressure release.
5. Repeat this 4 to 5 times.
6. Remove the portafilter and rinse everything.

This is the quick version I do regularly between full cleaning cycles.

## How often should you backflush a Breville Barista Express?

A good routine is:

- **Water backflush:** every 1 to 2 weeks
- **Tablet backflush / cleaning cycle:** about every 2 to 4 weeks, depending on use
- **Heavier daily use:** more often if you make many milk drinks or multiple shots every day

If the **Clean Me** light turns on, do a full cleaning cycle soon.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Here are a few common mistakes that can make backflushing less effective:

### Using too much cleaner
One tablet is enough. Using extra cleaner does not help and just creates more residue to rinse out.

### Not running a rinse cycle afterward
Always do a water-only rinse cycle after using detergent or a cleaning tablet.

### Waiting too long between cleanings
Coffee oils build up faster than many people expect. A regular routine makes cleaning easier.

### Forgetting the steam wand and drip tray
Backflushing cleans the brew path, but it does not replace cleaning the steam wand, drip tray, or water tank.

## Does backflushing fix bad espresso taste?

Sometimes, yes.

If your espresso tastes bitter, stale, or oddly dirty even with fresh beans and a normal grind setting, oil buildup inside the group head may be contributing to the problem.

Backflushing will not solve every espresso issue, but it often helps restore cleaner flavor if the machine is overdue for maintenance.

## Final thoughts

If you own a Breville Barista Express, learning how to backflush it properly is one of the easiest ways to keep the machine working well and your espresso tasting better.

It only takes a few minutes, and when done regularly, it helps prevent a lot of buildup that can affect both flavor and performance.

If you have not done it in a while, start with a full cleaning tablet cycle, then keep up with quick water backflushes in between.
